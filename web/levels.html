Levels

Which characters you own, and how far you got with them

Tick the characters you own and set each one's level (0–3: 0 = not levelled yet, 3 = done). The priorities pages only rank characters you own. Once set up, levels are kept up to date automatically: on the end-of-match scoreboard the toolkit reads the level badge of your row and corrects the character you just played (and marks it as owned).
